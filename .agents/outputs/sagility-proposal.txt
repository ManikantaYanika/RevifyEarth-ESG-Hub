information presented is complete, coherent, adequately substantiated and communicated in a manner appropriate for Sagility's stakeholders.


Having supported Sagility across previous reporting cycles, Revify will also ensure continuity with the Company's evolving sustainability narrative while identifying
opportunities to strengthen this year's disclosures.

                     Building a distinctive visual identity for the FY26 sustainability story

Video Report                                                                                                        Transforming a detailed sustainability report into an engaging
                                                                                                                    visual story



The Sustainability Video Report will provide a more accessible and engaging way to communicate Sagility's sustainability journey to stakeholders who may
not engage with the complete written report.

Rather than reproducing the report page by page, the video will distil its most important messages into a coherent 5–7 minute visual narrative. The
storytelling will be aligned with the final report theme, ensuring consistency across Sagility's print, digital and multimedia sustainability communication.

Sustainability Report Webpage Development
Complementary                                                                                             Extending the sustainability narrative into an accessible digital
                                                                                                          experience



As a complementary component of the proposed engagement, Revify will develop a dedicated digital webpage that presents the key highlights of Sagility's
FY26 Sustainability Report in an accessible and visually engaging format.

The webpage will not simply host a downloadable PDF. It will provide stakeholders with a concise digital gateway to Sagility's sustainability performance and
direct users to the complete report for detailed disclosures.
